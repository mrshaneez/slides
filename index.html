How it works

  1. Attach a document, paste your information, or both. PDFs are sent to the AI whole, so Dhivehi text, tables and scanned pages are read as they look. Word, PowerPoint and text files are copied into the box first so you can edit them.
  2. The AI writes the slides in formal Dhivehi, choosing bullet, card, number and section layouts.
  3. It only uses what you give it. It's told not to invent laws, article numbers or figures, but check every slide before you present.
  4. The slides open in Edit slides, where you can change any text, then download the PowerPoint. It comes out right-to-left with your heading and body fonts.

  • PowerPoint reads Thaana left-to-right unless the paragraph says rtl="1". That is why the first letter shows up last.
  • Fonts are set for both Latin and complex script, because PowerPoint draws Thaana with the complex-script font.
  • The theme's Thaana fallback font is swapped from MV Boli to your chosen fonts.
  • Wording, layout, colours and images are left as they are.
